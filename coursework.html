RELEVANT COURSEWORK

Coursework

Selected courses most relevant to my interests in digital hardware, embedded systems, and computer architecture.

ECE 4750: Computer Architecture

Processor microarchitecture, pipelining, caches and memory hierarchies, branch prediction, out-of-order and superscalar execution, virtual memory, multicore systems, cache coherence, and on-chip networks; RTL design and evaluation of processor systems.

ECE 2300: Digital Logic

Digital circuit and processor design using Verilog and FPGAs, including Boolean logic, combinational and sequential circuits, finite-state machines, memories, processor datapaths, and timing analysis.

ECE 3140: Embedded Systems

Embedded systems design with an emphasis on hardware/software interaction, including ARM assembly, interrupts and I/O, concurrency, locks and semaphores, process scheduling, priorities, resource management, and real-time constraints.

ECE 4760: Digital System Design: Microcontrollers

Real-time embedded systems design using the RP2350 microcontroller, applying concepts in interrupts, concurrency, scheduling, synchronization, and hardware/software interfacing through hands-on projects.

ECE 2400: Computer Systems Programming

Systems programming in C and C++, including data structures and algorithms, memory management, object-oriented and generic programming, concurrency, and POSIX system interfaces.

ECE 2100: Electronic Circuits

Analysis and design of analog circuits, including RLC networks, transient and frequency response, op-amps, transistor amplifiers, and switching power converters; hands-on circuit testing with oscilloscopes and other laboratory instrumentation.

ECE 3150: Microelectronics

Semiconductor devices and analog circuit design, including diodes, MOSFETs, BJTs, large- and small-signal models, current mirrors, single-stage and differential amplifiers, frequency response, and CMOS logic; reinforced through circuit analysis and hands-on labs.

ECE 4560: Power Electronics

Analysis, modeling, and design of switching power converters, including steady-state and dynamic behavior, power semiconductor devices, magnetic and thermal modeling, EMI filter design, and converter control.

ECE 3250: Signals and Systems

Continuous- and discrete-time signal and system analysis, including convolution, Fourier series and transforms, sampling theory, DFT/FFT, frequency-domain analysis, and spectrograms, with Python-based computational analysis and visualization.
