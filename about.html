ABOUT ME

Why I Love Engineering

Hello! I’m Catherine Cheng, an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Cornell University with a particular interest in digital hardware and embedded systems. I’m drawn to the process of designing and verifying the logic that makes a system work, whether that means developing RTL for an FPGA-based processor, writing low-level software for a microcontroller, or bringing hardware and software together in an embedded system.

What I find compelling about engineering is that nothing just works. Getting from an idea to a functioning system means understanding what is happening underneath, testing assumptions, finding the places where things break, and figuring out why. I especially enjoy that process of designing, debugging, and iterating until something behaves the way I intended.

I also like that engineering gives me a reason to learn about other things as well. Building something often starts with a technical problem but leads somewhere completely different: synthesizing birdsong means learning what makes those sounds distinctive, and working on autonomous drones can lead to learning how wind, air density, and other atmospheric conditions affect flight. Each new subject gives me something interesting to build around, and the process of building it makes me a better engineer.

This site is a collection of the projects and experiences that have shaped my engineering interests. Use the navigation bar to explore my projects, relevant coursework, and resume. If you'd like to discuss my work or potential opportunities, feel free to reach out!
