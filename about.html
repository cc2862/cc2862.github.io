ABOUT ME

Why Engineering?

Hello! I’m Catherine Cheng, an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Cornell University interested in digital hardware, embedded systems, and electrical system design.

What draws me to engineering is that nothing just works. Behind every system is a chain of decisions about how things communicate, how hardware behaves, what happens when assumptions break, and how all the pieces come together. I find the process of understanding and working with those details incredibly rewarding.

I also see engineering as more than a way to solve engineering problems. In the same way that learning to read gives you a tool for exploring almost any subject, engineering gives me a way to explore things I might never encounter otherwise. A project can start with a microcontroller or an FPGA and end with me learning about birdsong, autonomous flight, sensor systems, or space exploration. In the process of learning about those things, I become a better engineer too.

That curiosity has led me to work with computer architecture, embedded systems, digital design, electronics, and autonomous systems, including industry experience at Northrop Grumman. This site is a collection of the projects and experiences that have shaped those interests.
