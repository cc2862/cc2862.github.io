ABOUT ME

A Little More About Me

Hello! I’m Catherine Cheng, an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Cornell University with interests spanning digital hardware, embedded systems, and electrical system design. I enjoy working across the hardware–software boundary, from designing RTL and FPGA-based processors to developing real-time embedded systems and integrating electrical hardware for autonomous drones.

My engineering experience includes projects in computer architecture, embedded systems, digital design, and electronics, as well as industry experience at Northrop Grumman. I’m especially drawn to work where I can combine low-level design with hands-on implementation, testing, and debugging.

This site is a collection of the projects and experiences that have shaped my engineering interests. Use the navigation bar to explore my projects, relevant coursework, and resume. If you'd like to discuss my work or potential opportunities, feel free to reach out!
